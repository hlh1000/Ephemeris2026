I wanted an app that only held particular information, conveniently, so I made this html app with Gemini. It pulls swiss ephemeris data, adjusts for your location on a day-to-day viewer, and displays ephemeris table data for any month in GMT. You can tap the row and it displays superscript data, instead of referring higher up on the table, as on a ephemeris document.

It's kind of in response to how much work I did mentally the past year in particular. I got less interested in chart techniques and more in abstracting from simpler facts (e.g. an ingress means all month charts will have that signature). I had such a good idea of where things were I didn't look at charts very often. So I wanted something I could open and springboard into doing all the more detailed mental work without charting, e.g. the ASC, days of ingress, speed, exact degree, as well as putting ephemeris and local momentary data together, something you have to reach for, either between apps, thru multiple tabs, or setting multiple instances to run each. I was curious enough to put what data I wanted comfortably together. 

Because it pulls swiss ephemeris data by api, it should be totally accurate rather than approximate, if it doesn't break. The app uses your location for the day view table.

You can pull a Tarot Card by pushing the Tarot Draw button. Every card will list in a row until you press Refresh. Press Refresh to shuffle the deck. The pip cards are assigned decan signification.
